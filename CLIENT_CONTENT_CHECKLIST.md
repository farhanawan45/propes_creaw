# Props & Crew — Client Content Checklist

The current website is a design/demo build. Replace the items below before public launch.

## Brand assets

- Official logo in SVG format
- Horizontal and compact logo variants
- Brand guidelines, approved colours and fonts
- Final favicon and social image (1200 × 630 px)

## Contact details

- Business phone and WhatsApp numbers
- Enquiry email address
- Complete business address
- Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn URLs

The current phone numbers and Queen Street address in `src/content/site.ts` are demo values.

## Company credentials

- Verified events-delivered figure
- Verified years of experience
- Approval of all 14 service names
- Approval of the “100% New Zealand Coverage” claim
- Final About copy

## Projects and case studies

For every project provide:

- Title, category, location and date/year
- Approved photographs and videos
- Project overview and services delivered
- Verified guest/delegate count and duration
- Measurable result or client outcome
- Permission to publish names, logos and media

All six current projects and their figures are demo content until verified.

## Testimonials and client logos

- Testimonial text
- Customer name, title and company
- Approved profile photograph
- Client logos as SVG or transparent PNG
- Written publication permission

## Hero media

- Client-owned 10–20 second landscape showreel
- Web-ready MP4/WebM versions
- Matching poster image
- Confirmation of public usage rights

Current hero footage is licensed stock used for the demo.

## Forms and integrations

- SMTP host, port, username and password
- Receiving enquiry email
- Google Analytics GA4 measurement ID
- Google Search Console access
- CAPTCHA/Cloudflare Turnstile keys, if required

Secrets must be added directly to hosting environment variables and never committed to GitHub.

## Legal approval

- Review and approval of Privacy Policy
- Review and approval of Terms of Service
- Confirmed data-retention period
- Company legal name and registration details, if required
